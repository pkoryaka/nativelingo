Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NativeLingo really 100% free for personal use? ▼

Yes, completely. Under our open EULA, personal, educational, and open-source development use is 100% free forever. You can configure your own free Google Gemini API key (generous free rate limit) or connect directly to local Ollama with zero token costs.

How does the Smart Reply mode work? ▼

Highlight any message or conversation thread in Slack, Teams, Outlook, or your browser, then press Ctrl + Alt + R. A modal appears, determines the incoming message's language and tone, lets you specify what you want to say (or click a quick preset), drafts a polished response in a non-editable preview window, and automatically copies it to your clipboard.

Does NativeLingo collect or store my clipboard data? ▼

Never. NativeLingo operates zero cloud servers, zero analytics trackers, and zero telemetry. All text processing occurs locally on your machine or transmits directly via encrypted TLS to the provider endpoint you explicitly configure in Settings.

How do enterprise IT teams deploy NativeLingo across workstations? ▼

Administrators distribute a central policy.json file to %ProgramData%\NativeLingo\policy.json via Microsoft Intune or Group Policy (GPO). This automatically locks allowed AI endpoints, disables cloud egress if desired, and injects commercial license tokens without requiring individual employee configuration.
