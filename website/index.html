FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

Does NativeLingo transmit my clipboard or text to your servers?

No, never. NativeLingo has zero proprietary proxy servers, zero telemetry collectors, and zero user tracking. Text is transmitted directly from your workstation to your chosen AI provider (Gemini, Claude, Groq, or internal Ollama server) using your own API credentials.

Can I use NativeLingo completely offline?

Yes. Simply select Ollama or LM Studio in the AI Provider settings. You can run models like Llama 3.2, DeepSeek-R1, or Mistral 100% locally on your machine with zero internet connection required.

How do commercial licenses and tax receipts work?

Commercial users receive a cryptographically signed offline key (`NL1-...`). Formal PDF corporate invoices with full VAT/tax reverse charges and company details are delivered promptly via licensing@businessintelsystem.com.

How is NativeLingo different from DeepL Desktop?

DeepL charges a mandatory monthly fee per seat and only supports their proprietary cloud model.
