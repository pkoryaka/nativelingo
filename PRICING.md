> **Philosophy**: Zero hidden subscriptions, zero cloud relay surveillance, and zero markup on AI tokens. Individual personal and educational use is 100% free of charge. Commercial entities evaluating for workplace productivity receive a fully functional 40-day evaluation, after which commercial licensing applies.

## 1. Licensing Model & EULA Alignment

NativeLingo follows a **Fair-Code / Dual-Use Commercial Model** governed by the [NativeLingo EULA](./LICENSE):

1. **Personal, Educational & Non-Commercial Use (100% Free Forever • EULA Sec. 3)**:
   - Individual human users have a perpetual, royalty-free license to use the Software for private productivity, study, research, learning, and nonprofit activities across an unlimited number of devices.
   - **All features are fully unlocked** (instant in-place auto-paste, all 3 rewrite slots, jargon explainer, local Ollama and Gemini BYOK). No expiration, no payment required.
2. **Commercial & Workplace Deployment (40-Day Evaluation • EULA Sec. 2 & 4)**:
   - Commercial entities (corporations, businesses, paid client services, and government bodies) receive a **fully functional 40-day evaluation period** solely to assess internal suitability.
   - Continued commercial use after 40 days requires purchasing an official **Single-User Commercial** or **Multi-User Team License**.

## 4. The $0 Inference Markup Guarantee (BYOK)

NativeLingo never bills per-token or acts as a middleman for your data:
- **Google Gemini Cloud**: Google provides an exceptionally generous free tier for Gemini API keys (up to 15 requests per minute at zero cost). Even on paid pay-as-you-go tiers, typical daily translation costs less than $0.05 per month.
- **100% Offline Local AI**: Run open-source models via Ollama or LM Studio directly on your hardware (e.g., Llama 3, Qwen 2.5, Mistral, Gemma). Zero data leaves your machine, zero cost, 100% private.
